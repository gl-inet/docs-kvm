# How to use the cloud-based share device feature

The cloud-based share device is a premium feature that lets you grant other users remote access to your KVM devices and manage their access permissions remotely.

**Note:** Please upgrade your KVM firmware to version 1.7 before using this feature. 

Follow the steps below to use the cloud-based share devices feature.

1. Bind your KVM device to the Cloud. See [Bind your KVM to the Cloud](../faq/remote_access_via_cloud.md#bind-your-kvm-to-the-cloud) for details.

    ![cloud login](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/kvm/tutorials/how_to_use_share_devices/cloud_login.png){class="glboxshadow"}

2. After binding, log in to [https://glkvm.com/](https://glkvm.com/){target="_blank"}. Your bound devices will appear in the device list. Ensure the devices are online.

    ![device list](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/kvm/tutorials/how_to_use_share_devices/device_list.png){class="glboxshadow"}

3. Click the three-dot menu on the device card, and click **Share Device**.

    ![share device 1](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/kvm/tutorials/how_to_use_share_devices/share_devices_1.png){class="glboxshadow"}

4. Enter the email or username of the registered user who you want to share with, then click **Invite**.

    ![share device 2](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/kvm/tutorials/how_to_use_share_devices/share_devices_2.png){class="glboxshadow"}

    ![share device 3](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/kvm/tutorials/how_to_use_share_devices/share_devices_3.png){class="glboxshadow"}

5. On the recipient’s cloud platform page, the Device Sharing Invitation dialog pops up. Click **Accept**.

    ![accept share](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/kvm/tutorials/how_to_use_share_devices/accept_share.png){class="glboxshadow"}

    Once accepted, the recipient can access the shared device by clicking the share device.

    ![access device](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/kvm/tutorials/how_to_use_share_devices/access_device.png){class="glboxshadow"}

6.  You can see the successful sharing status on the cloud platform page.

    ![share successfully](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/kvm/tutorials/how_to_use_share_devices/share_successfully.png){class="glboxshadow"}

    To end sharing, click **Terminate**. Once terminated, the recipient will no longer have access to the shared device.

    ![terminate](https://static.gl-inet.com/docs/kvm/tutorials/how_to_use_share_devices/terminate.png){class="glboxshadow"}
